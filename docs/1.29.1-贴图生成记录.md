# 1.29.1 贴图生成记录

使用内置 ImageGen 的 generate 模式生成三个原创图集。风格参考 GregTech 与 Applied Energistics 的金属边框、铆钉、石英灰、紫色线路及青色指示灯，不复制外部资源文件。

安装到游戏的 PNG 是最终资源，均为 32×32。`tools/texture/import_gt_ae_atlas.py` 只做网格裁切、最近邻缩小和透明度阈值处理；UI 九宫格由程序绘制。共享终端六面沿用原方向序号（下 1、上 2、北 3、南 4、西 5、东 6），图集的方向文字仅作为生成时布局指令，以安装脚本映射为准。

旧 `GenTextures.java` 和 `gen_*_textures.py` 是历史占位纹理工具，勿对新版资源重新运行。废弃的整张 GUI 背景图已移除。

## 方块图集

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Minecraft 1.7.10 industrial mod block texture atlas, a single 4 by 4 contact sheet. Generate exactly 16 contiguous square texture tiles, equal size, with NO margins, NO gutters, NO text, NO labels. Entire image square 1024x1024. Pixel art deliberately drawn on a coarse 32x32 pixel grid in each tile (each logical pixel enlarged), nearest-neighbor crisp edges, no gradients, no antialiasing, no perspective. Style: classic GregTech metal machines and Applied Energistics 2 quartz iron panels, riveted charcoal frame, pale grey brushed metal, restrained cyan or purple LEDs. All block textures fill their square entirely, fully opaque. Row 1 tiles left to right: shared storage terminal vented side panel; terminal top with purple circuit socket; terminal north face with small central cyan storage screen and ONE glowing top indicator dot; terminal south face same shape with TWO top indicator dots. Row 2: terminal east face THREE dots; terminal west face FOUR dots; terminal upper face FIVE dots; terminal lower face SIX dots. Row 3: loot selection machine front with warm amber ticket slot and purple status light; loot machine side with industrial vents; loot machine top service hatch; IO node central core metallic plate with violet center. Row 4: IO connection arm neutral iron strip; IO connection end cap teal luminous socket; AE wireless signal transmitter with purple wireless emitter rings on quartz grey casing; ME super dock front overlay with square dark container docking aperture and purple AE circuit corners. Render ONLY the atlas of flat usable texture tiles, no surrounding objects or mockup.
```

## 物品图集

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Minecraft 1.7.10 inventory sprite atlas, SINGLE 4 columns x 2 rows sheet. Exactly 8 centered item sprites in equal square cells on genuine transparent background, NO text, NO labels, NO grid lines, no shadow outside silhouette. Pixel art like GregTech 16x16/32x32 mod inventory icons and Applied Energistics 2 components, crisp coarse square pixels, no antialiasing or smooth painting, strong silhouettes legible at 32 pixels. Square sheet with each sprite taking 80% of cell. Row 1 left to right: swift step amulet, silver framed cyan wing-shaped speed charm with gold chain; solar panel descaler tool, dark handle with iron scraper and small blue solar square; locator wand, bronze/iron shaft tipped with a bright emerald green scanning crystal and tiny cyan coils; minigame helper, small gold mechanical puzzle control device with distinct colored four-square face and iron border. Row 2: shared ITEM storage cell, AE2 white-grey rectangular storage cartridge with purple circuit border, emerald green storage stripe and tiny box glyph; shared FLUID storage cell, same matching cartridge with blue cyan stripe and clear droplet glyph; AE wireless receiver cover, square thin GregTech iron machine cover with purple wireless arcs and dark central chip; settings gear icon, silver eight-tooth cog with violet center. Transparent silhouettes, common upper-left highlight, tightly limited charcoal iron quartz-grey palette with colored functional accents. No 3D perspective mockup, render only the actual usable pixel-art inventory sprites.
```

## 拆解机图集

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one Minecraft block texture strip with exactly TWO flat square tiles side by side, no border outside tiles, no gutter, no text. Each tile is coarse pixel art designed for a 32x32 texture, crisp square pixels without antialiasing, classic GregTech grey LV machine casing with dark iron rim and four corner screws. Machine is a single-step disassembler: central square input aperture, below it two branching amber/copper reverse crafting chevrons pointing outwards toward small separate output sockets, restrained cyan circuit details. Left tile is inactive with dark aperture and dim amber indicators; right tile is the identical layout running active with lit cyan aperture and bright amber chevrons. Front faces only, strictly orthographic flat texture artwork, entirely opaque. No perspective, no illustrated whole cubes, no labels, no smooth painting.
```
